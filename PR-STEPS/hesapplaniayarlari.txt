**Turkish accounting research summary (for your requested logic)**
- `180/280`, `380/480`, `181/281`, `381/481` are explicitly defined in Tek Düzen and long/short transfers are part of year/period-end control.
- Practical flow:
  - Deferred revenue: `480 -> 380` as maturity gets within one year, then recognition to revenue.
  - Prepaid expense: `280 -> 180`, then amortization to expense.
  - Accrued revenue: `281 -> 181`, then clear on invoice/realization.
  - Accrued expense: `481 -> 381`, then clear on invoice/payment.
